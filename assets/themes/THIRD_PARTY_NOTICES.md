Third-party themes vendored in this directory:

- `Catppuccin Mocha.tmTheme`: from `https://github.com/catppuccin/bat`, MIT License.
- `Sublime Snazzy.tmTheme`: from `https://github.com/greggb/sublime-snazzy`, MIT License.
- `Dracula.tmTheme`: from `https://github.com/dracula/sublime`, MIT License.

These files are embedded into the mdstream binary for runtime theme selection.
